FUN FACT / ORIGIN STORY

Benson says it began when he was about four: after seeing his dad backflip at a family barbecue, he tried one from the couch. He later became a competitive high-school diver, placed sixth at Washington’s state meet, and flipped as he left his 2021 American Idol audition. At one of his first tiny concerts, he threw a backflip because he wasn’t sure how else to work the crowd. Fans loved it—so the move stayed and became his live trademark.
